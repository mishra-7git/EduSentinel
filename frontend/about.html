ABOUT EDUSENTINEL

Protecting the Integrity of Examinations

EduSentinel is a digital platform designed to create a structured and secure channel for reporting suspected examination paper leaks and other examination-related irregularities.

THE PROBLEM

Why EduSentinel?

Examination paper leaks can affect students, institutions and the credibility of the examination system.

Students or citizens may notice suspicious activities but often do not have a structured way to report them to the appropriate authorities.

EduSentinel aims to provide a centralized digital platform where suspected incidents can be reported, reviewed and tracked.

PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY

Your Identity Matters

EduSentinel is designed so that reporter identity and personal information are not displayed publicly through complaint tracking.

Access to sensitive reporter information should be restricted to authorized officials when required for legitimate investigation.
